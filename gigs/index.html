RDU DJSOC Takeover

You'll probably see me bi-weekly every Friday 12-2 spining music ranging from disco, dance, drum and bass, and bits and bobs in-between. Don't expect anything near pop, but expect to be able to boogie for a solid two hours to some solid electronic music. You can tune in on 98.5FM or on their website. I don't always bother to put my tracklist's here but if you ask for an ID I'll always provide.
